SIEM and XDR Alert Triage

Monitoring suspicious activity in a 24/7 environment, validating SIEM and Cortex XDR alerts, and escalating potential incidents within agreed SLA requirements across more than 500 endpoints.

Context

The operational environment required continuous monitoring of security alerts generated through SIEM and XDR platforms across more than 500 endpoints.

Responsibility

I worked in 24/7 shift operations performing security-alert monitoring, triage, and validation.

  • Reviewed SIEM and XDR alerts for suspicious activity.
  • Validated relevant context before escalation.
  • Escalated potential incidents for further investigation and response.
  • Worked within agreed SLA requirements.
  • Supported consistent monitoring across a large endpoint estate.

Approach

The role required disciplined handling of alert queues: review the signal, gather available context, determine whether activity warranted further investigation, and document or escalate according to the operational process.

OPERATING PRINCIPLEAlert volume is not the same as incident volume; validation is necessary before escalation.

Outcome

Monitoring and validation activities were performed consistently within the SLA across the stated endpoint coverage. The source resume does not provide false-positive rates, mean time to acknowledge, or incident counts, so those metrics are not invented.

What this demonstrates

  • Experience in 24/7 SOC shift operations.
  • SIEM and XDR alert handling.
  • Security-event analysis and escalation judgment.
  • Ability to work against operational service levels.

No alert screenshots, detection logic, incident records, client details, endpoint identifiers, or response procedures are included.
